A practical improvement cycle

The E.D.G.E. is in what we do next.

No one-size-fits-all fixes. A collaborative process that meets each school where it is and builds toward lasting progress.

E 01 / EVALUATE

Understand the need

Listen, review relevant data, and identify strengths and priorities.

D 02 / DESIGN

Choose the direction

Set clear goals and shape a focused plan with the people doing the work.

G 03 / GROW

Build capacity

Put the plan into practice with coaching, collaboration, and useful tools.

E 04 / EXCEL

Learn and sustain

Monitor progress, adjust what is needed, and strengthen what works.

Listen & learn → Design & implement → Monitor & adjust → Repeat

Meet the educator behind the edge.

Dr. Julon K. Eatmon is an educator and instructional leader with more than two decades of experience in mathematics instruction, curriculum, assessment, and school improvement.

Her work centers on helping educators make sense of student data and decide what to do next. At Huffman High School, her collaborative work supported a six-point increase in the school's state report-card score over two years.
